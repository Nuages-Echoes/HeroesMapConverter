TREES INVENTORY - Heroes II (fh2m) and Heroes III (h3m)
==============================================================================

Characteristics per tree: ID, footprint, land type, visual species.
Footprint: blocked cells as (dx, dy), normalized to the NW corner of
the shape ('(passable)' = no blocked cell). The h3m object list is
the game's own Objects.txt (RoE + AB + SoD additions); footprints
decoded from its passability bitmaps and verified against the
reference-map catalog (all match). A '*' marks the defs already
in the converter's catalog. H3 land types come from the def name
suffix (sn=snow, d=dirt, g=grass, r=rough, s=swamp, u=subterranean)
and, for unsuffixed families, from the VCMI engine biome templates.
